Predator Type — Scene Queen

Mark Floros

He doesn't hunt so much as receive. That's the whole trick of running a room like this — the room does the work, and all Mark has to do is stand somewhere with good light and let people find reasons to talk to him.

Tonight it's a woman in from Deansgate who's had two glasses of something expensive and is telling him, with real conviction, that meeting him is the best thing that's happened to her this year. He believes she believes it. That's rather the point of a place like this — everyone here wants to feel like tonight is the night something happens to them.

He could feed on anyone in this room. He's picky about it anyway, and not for any reason he could easily explain if asked. There's a specific charge he's after — that particular electric, wanting quality some people carry, the sense that they'd say yes to anything before they'd finished being asked. Tonight it's her, practically vibrating with the thrill of being noticed by him specifically.

He leans in close, murmurs something about the private room upstairs, and watches her decide — instantly, gladly — that of course she'll come.

Upstairs, low light, the noise of the club gone soft through the floor. She doesn't flinch when he tips her head aside, doesn't resist at all — if anything she leans into it, breath already quickening like this is the moment she came out tonight hoping for. He finds the pulse at her throat and drinks, and it hits exactly the way he wanted it to: bright, urgent, greedy for more of whatever's about to happen next. It's a good vintage, as far as these things go. He takes enough to satisfy without taking so much she'll remember tomorrow as anything but a very good night.

When he straightens, she's already smiling, glassy and pleased, already forgetting the specifics the way they always do. He fixes his cuffs and feels it settle into him — something sharper, more convincing, if that's even possible. He doesn't examine it too closely. Some things are better enjoyed than understood.
