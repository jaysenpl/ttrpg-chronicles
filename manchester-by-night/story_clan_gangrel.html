Clan

Gangrel

Bo doesn't walk so much as prowl, shoulders loose, head tracking every sound like it might be worth chasing. Three years of freedom and it still hasn't worn off, the sheer fact of not having to ask permission for anything — not where to be, not what time to be there, not who to become. They spent eighteen years being told exactly who to be. They're not spending eternity doing it too.

The campus at this hour belongs to security guards, and even they've mostly stopped bothering with this stretch. Good. Bo likes it best when there's nobody around to perform for.

"You're doing the thing again," says a voice behind them — Rae, another Anarch, unimpressed in the specific way only someone who's known you a while can manage.

"What thing?"

"The thing where you go looking for trouble because being bored feels worse than getting caught."

Bo grins, all teeth, and doesn't deny it. Somewhere under their skin something shifts — not fully, not enough to show, just a flicker, the animal part of them stretching like it's testing whether tonight's the night it gets to come out properly. It usually isn't. They're getting better at that. Mostly.

"I heard the Tower's got someone new patrolling near the halls," Bo says. "Thought I'd go say hello."

"That's Camarilla ground."

"Exactly."

Rae doesn't bother arguing. Nobody really does, not anymore — it's easier to just watch and hope tonight's the night Bo actually thinks something through before doing it, rather than after. It never is. Bo's already halfway across the quad, already grinning at nothing, already three steps ahead of any plan that might have talked them out of it.

Freedom, Bo's decided, isn't really about being able to do anything. It's about nobody being able to stop you trying.
